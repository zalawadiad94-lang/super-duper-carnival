Laying notes

Set out from the centre

Colour sequence

  • Alternate Grey / White tile by tile so no two same-colour faces meet edge to edge.
  • Accra Stone ships in 4 faces — draw from several boxes at once so a face never repeats along a diagonal.
  • Dry-lay one full 3 m × 3 m bay and get it approved before fixing anything.

Joints & movement

  • 2 mm grout joint, as drawn. Widen to 3 mm if the tile is not rectified.
  • 10 mm perimeter gap at all walls and columns, kept free of adhesive and covered by skirting.
  • Movement joints at roughly 5 m centres both ways, carried full depth through the bed to the slab.

Bed & substrate

Fix on a cement-based tile adhesive over a level, fully cured screed. Back-butter and target better than 90% bed contact — hollow spots under a heavy-traffic floor turn into cracked tiles. Check slab level and deflection before the first tile goes down.

If this is a parking deck

The reference image is a parking floor. Confirm the tile's breaking strength and abrasion class carry vehicle wheel loads, and specify R10 or better slip resistance for a matt surface that will see tyre water. Bay markings can be run in Accra White as a continuous two-tile band instead of paint.

Alternative format

A 600 × 1200 plank in the same two colours suits a linear alternating-band floor and cuts the joint count dramatically — but it will not give the square-checker look in the reference. Larger squares also read better at parking scale; the size selector above compares them.
