# Accra Stone — 50′ × 55′ floor setting-out

Setting-out plan and material take-off for a checkerboard floor in **Accra Grey /
Accra White** (Accra Stone collection, matt) over a 50′-0″ × 55′-0″ area
(15240 × 16764 mm, 2,750 sq ft / 255.48 m²).

Open `accra-stone-50x55.html` in a browser. It is a single self-contained file.

## What it covers

- To-scale setting-out drawing with dimensions, centrelines, perimeter cut bands
  and movement joints.
- Grid arithmetic for 400 × 400, 600 × 600 and 800 × 800 at a 2 mm or 3 mm joint.
- Material take-off — tiles by colour, +10% allowance, box counts, coverage.
- Sundries: adhesive, grout, spacers, sealant.
- Laying notes.

## Headline figures — 400 × 400 mm at a 2 mm joint

| | |
|---|---|
| Grid | 38 × 42 = **1,596 tiles** |
| Split | 798 Accra Grey / 798 Accra White |
| Full (uncut) tiles | 1,440 — 90% |
| Perimeter band across 50′ | 383 mm each end (96% of a tile) |
| Perimeter band along 55′ | 341 mm each end (85% of a tile) |
| Order qty +10% | 1,756 tiles — 294 boxes at 6 pcs/box |

Packing in every course that fits (37 across, 41 deep) would leave 182 mm and
140 mm slivers at the walls. Dropping one course in each direction widens those
bands to 383 mm and 341 mm, which is why the grid is 38 × 42.

**Pattern scale:** a tile-by-tile checker at 400 mm reads much busier than the
reference image, whose squares are nearer 2.5 ft. The *2 × 2 Blocks* option
groups four tiles into an 800 mm visual square to match it — same tile, same
quantities.

Quantities are indicative; confirm box counts and coverage against the supplier's
product data sheets.
