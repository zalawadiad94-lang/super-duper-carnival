# Astrology reference

The interpretive vocabulary, for answering astrology questions without a fetch.
Explain these the way you'd explain any system of ideas — from inside it, with
specifics. Vague affirmations are what make astrology talk boring; the tradition
itself is precise, and precision is what the user came for.

## The twelve signs

Each entry: dates · element · modality · ruler · what it's said to do well ·
its shadow. The shadow matters — a reading that only flatters is the sort of
thing that gives horoscopes their reputation.

| Sign | Dates | El. | Mod. | Ruler | Strengths | Shadow |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Aries | Mar 21–Apr 19 | Fire | Cardinal | Mars | Initiative, nerve, momentum | Impatience, picking fights, starting over finishing |
| Taurus | Apr 20–May 20 | Earth | Fixed | Venus | Steadiness, taste, endurance | Stubbornness, inertia, possessiveness |
| Gemini | May 21–Jun 20 | Air | Mutable | Mercury | Curiosity, wit, adaptability | Scattered, restless, glib |
| Cancer | Jun 21–Jul 22 | Water | Cardinal | Moon | Care, memory, loyalty | Moodiness, clinging, indirectness |
| Leo | Jul 23–Aug 22 | Fire | Fixed | Sun | Warmth, generosity, presence | Pride, needing the room, drama |
| Virgo | Aug 23–Sep 22 | Earth | Mutable | Mercury | Discernment, craft, service | Perfectionism, criticism, worry |
| Libra | Sep 23–Oct 22 | Air | Cardinal | Venus | Fairness, charm, diplomacy | Indecision, conflict-avoidance, people-pleasing |
| Scorpio | Oct 23–Nov 21 | Water | Fixed | Pluto (trad. Mars) | Depth, focus, loyalty under pressure | Control, suspicion, grudges |
| Sagittarius | Nov 22–Dec 21 | Fire | Mutable | Jupiter | Vision, humour, appetite for the new | Tactlessness, over-promising, flight |
| Capricorn | Dec 22–Jan 19 | Earth | Cardinal | Saturn | Discipline, patience, the long game | Coldness, workaholism, rigidity |
| Aquarius | Jan 20–Feb 18 | Air | Fixed | Uranus (trad. Saturn) | Originality, principle, perspective | Aloofness, contrarianism, abstraction |
| Pisces | Feb 19–Mar 20 | Water | Mutable | Neptune (trad. Jupiter) | Empathy, imagination, forgiveness | Escapism, blurred boundaries, martyrdom |

Modern rulers are listed first; traditional rulers still matter in Hellenistic
and traditional practice, so name both when a user is working in that idiom.

## Elements and modalities

**Elements** — the temperament.
- **Fire** (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): action, faith, enthusiasm. Warms a room; burns out.
- **Earth** (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): material, practical, patient. Builds; resists change.
- **Air** (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): ideas, language, distance. Connects; abstracts away from feeling.
- **Water** (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): feeling, intuition, merging. Attunes; loses the edges.

**Modalities** — the tempo, where a sign sits in its season.
- **Cardinal** (Aries, Cancer, Libra, Capricorn): starts things; season openers.
- **Fixed** (Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, Aquarius): sustains things; season middles.
- **Mutable** (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces): adapts and ends things; season closers.

Element plus modality nearly defines a sign, and it's the most useful thing to
reach for when explaining why two signs behave alike or clash. Fixed signs
square each other (Taurus–Leo–Scorpio–Aquarius) — that's the classic
immovable-object friction.

## The big three

Asked "what's my sign", most people mean the sun. Astrologers usually want three:

- **Sun** — the core self, what you're becoming. Determined by birth date alone.
- **Moon** — the inner emotional life, needs, what soothes you. Needs birth date
  and roughly the time; the moon changes sign every ~2.5 days.
- **Rising / Ascendant** — the mask, first impressions, and the frame the whole
  chart hangs on. Needs **exact time and place** — it changes sign roughly every
  two hours.

This is why "just tell me my rising sign from my birthday" can't be answered.
Ask for time and city and point them at a calculator
(see `sources.md`) rather than estimating — a wrong ascendant misplaces every
house in the chart, so a guess isn't a rough answer, it's a different chart.

## Planets

- **Sun** — identity, vitality, purpose
- **Moon** — emotion, instinct, what you need to feel safe
- **Mercury** — thinking, speech, learning, commerce
- **Venus** — love, beauty, values, what you're drawn to
- **Mars** — drive, anger, desire, how you fight
- **Jupiter** — growth, luck, meaning, excess
- **Saturn** — limits, discipline, time, the lessons that stick
- **Uranus** — disruption, invention, sudden change (generational, ~7 yrs/sign)
- **Neptune** — dream, illusion, spirituality, dissolution (~14 yrs/sign)
- **Pluto** — power, death and rebirth, the buried (~12–20 yrs/sign)

The three outer planets move so slowly that their sign is shared by everyone
born within years of you — so their *house* and *aspects* carry the personal
meaning, not the sign. Worth saying when someone reads significance into
"Pluto in Scorpio".

## Houses

Twelve life areas the planets fall into. Which house a planet occupies depends
on birth time, which is why chart work needs it.

| House | Domain |
|---|---|
| 1st | Self, body, appearance, how you arrive |
| 2nd | Money, possessions, self-worth |
| 3rd | Communication, siblings, short trips, early learning |
| 4th | Home, roots, family, the private self |
| 5th | Creativity, romance, children, play |
| 6th | Work, routine, health, service |
| 7th | Partnership, marriage, open enemies |
| 8th | Shared resources, intimacy, death, transformation |
| 9th | Travel, philosophy, higher study, belief |
| 10th | Career, reputation, public role |
| 11th | Friends, groups, hopes, networks |
| 12th | The unconscious, solitude, what's hidden, undoing |

Houses 1/7, 2/8, 3/9 etc. sit opposite and work as pairs — self and other, mine
and ours, my view and the wider world.

## Aspects

Angles between planets. The five majors:

- **Conjunction (0°)** — fused; the two functions act as one, for better or worse.
- **Sextile (60°)** — easy, opportunity, needs a nudge to activate.
- **Square (90°)** — friction, tension, the productive kind of problem.
- **Trine (120°)** — flowing, talented, so easy it can go unused.
- **Opposition (180°)** — polarised; resolved by integration, not by choosing a side.

Squares and oppositions are where growth is said to live. A chart of only trines
is comfortable and often inert — worth saying when someone reads a hard aspect
as bad news.

## Compatibility

Same-element pairs (Fire–Fire, Water–Water) are easy and can be stagnant.
Complementary elements — Fire–Air, Earth–Water — are the classic strong matches:
air feeds fire, water makes earth fertile. Fire–Water and Earth–Air are the
harder combinations: steam and dust, though "harder" means more work, not doomed.

Same-modality pairs clash on tempo. Two cardinal signs both want to lead; two
fixed signs neither will move; two mutable signs may never settle.

Sun-sign compatibility is the shallowest layer, and worth saying so when a user
asks whether they're doomed with an Aries. Venus (what you want) and Mars (how
you pursue it) are the placements astrologers actually read for attraction, and
the 7th house for partnership. Frame compatibility as *how* two temperaments
tend to grate or fit, never as a verdict on a real relationship.

## Transits and cycles

The ones people ask about:

- **Mercury retrograde** — ~3× a year, ~3 weeks each. Traditionally: review,
  revise, expect delays and miscommunication; a poor time to sign or launch, a
  good one to redo. Whether it's currently retrograde is a dated fact — look it
  up, don't recall it.
- **Saturn return** — Saturn returns to its natal position around ages 29–30,
  again ~58–59. Read as the reckoning that ends an extended youth: career,
  commitment, and what you'll actually be responsible for.
- **Jupiter return** — every ~12 years; expansion, luck, appetite.
- **Eclipses** — twice-yearly seasons; accelerated endings and beginnings along
  the affected axis.
- **Retrogrades of Venus** (~every 18 months) and **Mars** (~every 2 years) —
  read as reviews of relationship and of drive respectively.
- **New moon / full moon** — new for setting intentions, full for culmination
  and release. Monthly; the sign it falls in colours it.

For anything about the current sky — where the moon is today, whether a
retrograde is active, when the next eclipse falls — look it up. Your training
has a cutoff; the sky doesn't.
